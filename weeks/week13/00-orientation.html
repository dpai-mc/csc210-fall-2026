Week 13 — The Whole Machine

The program counter, the instruction register and your RAM join the bus; fetch; your first program  |  11/09 – 11/15  |  Estimated time: ~7.75 hours

Nobody sets ir any more.

Last week’s control unit ran an instruction by itself, but only after you had typed that instruction into an input pin. You were still the part of the computer that decides what happens next. And your RAM, built in Week 8, has sat in its own tab ever since, connected to nothing.

This week three parts join your bus — a program counter, an instruction register and the RAM — and three steps join your control table. Then you put five bytes in memory, raise one pin, and click. The CPU reads an instruction, does it, reads the next, and does that. You will watch it compute 7 − 3 with no subtract instruction anywhere in it.
