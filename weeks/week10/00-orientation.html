Week 10 — One Bus, Four Registers

The CPU core: the bus, the register file, the parts around the ALU, and the instruction set  |  10/19 – 10/25  |  Estimated time: ~9 hours

Your parts have never met.

Your file holds a register, an ALU with its flags, and a RAM with its address register. Each one works, and each one sits in its own tab, connected to nothing. Last week ended on the picture of a real processor: a datapath that holds and moves values, and a control unit that decides when.

This week you build the datapath of your own CPU. One shared 8-bit bus, four registers a program can name, and the small parts the ALU needs around it to work on a bus at all. By Sunday you will run ADD R0, R1 by hand, in three moves, on a circuit you wired yourself. You are still the control unit — for one more week.
