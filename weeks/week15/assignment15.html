Part A — Canvas quiz (25 points)

Ten questions on the four Week 15 pages, 2.5 points each, auto-graded. No time limit. Two attempts; your higher score counts. Each question is drawn from two parallel versions, so a second attempt will usually look different. Opens Monday 11/23; closes Sunday 11/29, 11:59 pm. Answers are released Monday 11/30.

The questions follow the pages in order:

  1. time from instruction count, CPI and clock rate;
  2. the CPI of a mix of instructions;
  3. steps and ticks on your CPU, and which lever a change pulls;
  4. what sets the clock;
  5. Amdahl’s Law;
  6. cycles for a pipelined program;
  7. data or control hazard;
  8. macros and subroutines;
  9. the assembler’s symbol table;
  10. linker, loader or debugger.

Several answers are numbers. Have a calculator ready; the Performance Calculator on page 1 and the Amdahl Explorer on page 2 are the best practice.

Part B — Research topic (5 points, complete/incomplete)

In Week 16 you write a short paper on one feature of modern processors, connecting it to what you have learned in this course. Choosing the topic now means Week 16 is spent writing, not deciding.

Open the Research Topic page in this week’s module. Choose a topic (there is a list, or propose your own), write the one question your paper will answer, name two things from this course it connects to and two sources you plan to use, and say one thing about it you do not understand yet. Print it to PDF and upload it. It is complete when every section is answered, and you get written feedback either way, in time to change topic if you need to.
