Research Topic

5 points · complete/incomplete  |  Week 15, Part B  |  Due Sunday 11/29, 11:59 pm  |  Print to PDF and upload to Canvas

In Week 16 you write a short paper on one feature of modern processors, connecting it to what you have learned in this course. Week 16 gives the full requirements. This page is where you choose the feature and say what you want to find out, so that the feedback you get back can steer you before you start writing.

It is complete, and earns all 5 points, when every section is answered. Nothing here is graded on which topic you choose.

Nothing you type on this page is saved anywhere

There is no storage behind these boxes. Close the tab or reload and your typing is gone. Print to PDF before you leave.

You

1. Your topic

1a. Choose one, or choose “my own” and name it below. Each says where in this course it connects; you are not limited to those.

1b. Your topic in your own words, in one sentence. (If you chose your own, this is where you name it.)

2. Your question

2. The one question your paper will answer. A good question has an answer you do not know yet: “Why do processors predict branches instead of waiting?” works; “What is branch prediction?” is a definition, not a question.

3. The connection to this course

3. Two things from this course your topic connects to, by week, and one sentence on each connection. (For example: “Week 15, page 3: a mispredicted branch is the control hazard; the instructions fetched behind it are thrown away.”)

4. Sources

4. Two sources you plan to use, and who published each one (a textbook, a manufacturer, a university, a news site, …). Saylor CS301 counts as one.

5. What you do not understand yet

5. One thing about your topic you do not understand yet. This is what the feedback will try to help with.
