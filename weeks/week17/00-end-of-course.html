Week 17 — End of Course

Course synthesis  |  12/07 – 12/11  |  This week: your final project

From a voltage to a computer

In Week 1 a bit was a voltage that was either high or low. Seventeen weeks later, those bits make up a CPU you built gate by gate, and it runs programs from its own memory. This last week has no new content and nothing new to submit. It is for finishing that CPU and looking back at how you got there.

Your .circ file and your documentation PDF. The full requirements, the tiers and the submission checklist are on the Final Project page, repeated in this week’s module. It is the only thing due this week.
